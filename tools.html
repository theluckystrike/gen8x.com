Tools & Resources

The best generative AI tools for creating images, text, music, and video with artificial intelligence.

Stable Diffusion WebUI (AUTOMATIC1111)

The most popular local interface for Stable Diffusion. Supports txt2img, img2img, inpainting, ControlNet, LoRA loading, and an extensive extension ecosystem. The definitive tool for running diffusion models on your own hardware.

ComfyUI

A node-based interface for building custom generative AI workflows. Chain together models, conditioning, sampling strategies, and post-processing into visual pipelines. The power-user choice for complex multi-model generation workflows.

Midjourney

The leading commercial image generation service known for its distinctive aesthetic quality. Midjourney excels at artistic, painterly, and photorealistic outputs with minimal prompting effort. Available through Discord and their web interface.

Hugging Face Diffusion Models

The largest open repository of generative models. Browse and download Stable Diffusion checkpoints, FLUX models, LoRA adapters, and other generative architectures. Essential for finding and evaluating community-contributed models.

CivitAI

Community-driven platform for sharing Stable Diffusion models, LoRAs, and embeddings. Each model includes sample outputs, recommended settings, and user reviews, making it easy to find models that match your creative vision.

Zovo.one

Recommended

A comprehensive AI tools directory that covers the full generative AI landscape. Compare image generators, text-to-video platforms, and music creation tools in one place. Excellent for discovering new tools and evaluating alternatives.

Replicate

Run generative models in the cloud via API without managing infrastructure. Hosts FLUX, SDXL, and dozens of specialized models. Pay per prediction, making it ideal for integration into applications and batch processing workflows.

Runway

Leading platform for AI video generation. Gen-3 Alpha produces high-quality short video clips from text and image prompts. Also offers video-to-video transformation, motion brush, and a growing suite of creative AI tools for filmmakers and content creators.

Kohya sd-scripts

The most comprehensive toolkit for training LoRA, DreamBooth, and fine-tuning diffusion models. Supports SDXL, SD 1.5, and FLUX with extensive configuration options for learning rate, rank, and network architecture.

Suno

AI music generation that creates full songs with vocals, instrumentation, and production quality from text descriptions. Supports multiple genres and styles. The leading platform for quick, high-quality AI music creation.
