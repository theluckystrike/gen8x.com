About gen8x

gen8x.com is a free, client-side color palette generator built for developers and designers. Generate harmonious 5-color palettes from random seeds, hex codes, or keywords, then export them as CSS variables, Tailwind config, SCSS variables, or JSON.

Everything runs in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Your last 10 palettes are saved in localStorage so you can revisit them.
